Canadian Sectoral Savings Forecasting

A Canadian sectoral net-savings forecasting project using quarterly Statistics Canada and Bank of Canada data from 1977 Q1 to 2025 Q3.

Co-authored with Jingwen Leng, Junhao Wen, Shisheng Xiao, and Boyan Yu.

GitHubReport PDF

Sufficient statistics

The question
Can macro predictors improve household net-savings forecasts relative to persistence benchmarks?
The data
Quarterly Canadian net savings, income, consumption, unemployment, interest-rate, and CPI series from Statistics Canada and the Bank of Canada, harmonized from 1977 Q1 to 2025 Q3.
My part
I worked on the forecasting design, wrote reusable R helpers, and compared benchmark rules, ARIMA-family models, and smaller ARIMAX specifications under one-step and rolling RMSE.
What came out
R notebooks, a final PDF report, forecast-comparison figures, ACF/PACF and variogram diagnostics, RMSE tables, and renv-backed environment files.

Methods and stack

Methods
ARIMAX, log differencing, ADF tests, variograms, one-step RMSE, rolling windows, forecast RMSE, benchmark comparisons, stationarity diagnostics, sector comparison
Tech stack
R, renv, tidyverse, forecasting, Quarto/R Markdown
