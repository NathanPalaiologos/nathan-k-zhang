Causal ML · Nov 2025 - Dec 2025 · Course research project

Payment Friction and Spending: Double ML Evidence

Causal ML on payment friction and daily spending, estimated from consumer payment diaries.

Co-authored with Jerrold Huang, Jiaxun Gao, Xiangyu Zhang, and Mingyu Quan.

GitHubReport PDF

Sufficient statistics

The question
Are lower-friction payment methods associated with higher daily spending after controlling for demographics, timing, and basket composition?
The data
The 2024 Atlanta Fed Diary of Consumer Payment Choice, organized as 5,583 individuals and 22,332 in-person person-days, with four diary days per respondent.
My part
I helped define the person-day treatment and outcome, estimate the nuisance models, and handle the design constraints: non-random diary assignment and sparse demographic variation.
What came out
A reproducible notebook pipeline, a written report, DR-loss checks, respondent-level diagnostics, and CATE plots by ticket size, income, and education.

Methods and stack

Methods
Double ML, orthogonal scores, cross-fitting, tree-based nuisance models, DR-loss, CATE profiling
Tech stack
Python, pandas, scikit-learn, XGBoost, DoubleML
