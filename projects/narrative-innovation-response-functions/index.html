Financial econometrics · Jun 2026 - present · Working paper with Jerrold Huang

Narrative Innovation Response Functions

Turning disclosure text into out-of-fold narrative innovations, then studying what happens to abnormal returns around the event window.

Research project in progress with Jerrold Huang; for the website, I use the demo and aggregate results I can share.

Sufficient statistics

The question
When managers and analysts speak during earnings calls, which parts of the language still move with returns after the pre-call state and earlier dialogue are accounted for?
The data
Public SEC 8-K filings for the laptop demo; a 9,626-call WRDS S&P 500 earnings-call panel linked to Capital IQ transcripts, CRSP returns, IBES estimates, and Compustat/CRSP identifiers for the paper pipeline.
My part
I worked on the reusable `src/nirf` layer, the public 8-K demo, the WRDS S&P 500 staging pipeline, and the paper-freeze script that bundles baseline results, diagnostics, learner-audit tables, CSVs, TeX tables, and figures.
What came out
A laptop-runnable 8-K demo, a WRDS S&P 500 earnings-call pipeline, response curves, placebo and residualized-outcome checks, learner-audit tables, and a frozen paper-results package.

Methods and stack

Methods
out-of-fold narrative innovations, forward block splits, event-window CAR, clustered inference, learner-invariance audit, placebo windows, WRDS exporter
Tech stack
Python, scikit-learn, pandas, WRDS, CRSP, IBES, Capital IQ, LaTeX
