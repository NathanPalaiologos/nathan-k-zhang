Age-Biased Technology Exposure Data Pipeline

A data build for age-biased technological change: PSID panels, CPS wage surfaces, O*NET ability weights, and patent-to-ability text inputs.

Built with Dr. J. Frank Li and Dr. Cheng Chen at UBC Sauder.

Short version

What it was
Which occupations and workers are more exposed to technologies whose patent text lines up with O*NET ability requirements?
Data I touched
PSID 2003-2023 panels, CPS full-time wage samples, USPTO and EPO TIP/PATSTAT patent text and metadata, O*NET releases, OEWS 2024 wages/employment, and occupation crosswalks.
My part
I keep the labor side and text side close enough to debug together: PSID/O*NET linkage, CPS age-ability wage outputs, Phase I embedding inputs, Phase II model-specific embedding and similarity jobs, and the QA files that catch bad joins.
Where it landed
PSID and CPS analysis tables, SOC-level AB measures, O*NET ability weights, patent embedding inputs, sharded patent-to-ability similarity files, sample funnels, link diagnostics, and Excel/LaTeX tables for review.

Methods and stack

Methods
PatEmbed, PatentSBERTa, and Qwen embeddings, patent-to-ability similarity, O*NET ability weights, PSID/CPS panel construction, occupation crosswalks, Sockeye/Slurm jobs, schema and QA manifests
Tech stack
Python, R, Polars, Parquet, PatEmbed, PatentSBERTa, Qwen3, Slurm/HPC
