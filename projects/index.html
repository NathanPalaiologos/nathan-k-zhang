Memory Is the Difference between Two Futures: Memory Response Paths and Path-Structured Context Compilation

A memory can change the path after the next answer. With Jerrold Huang, I define that change by comparing matched futures with and without one memory, traced over four horizons in 180 independent ecologies. A separate 144-item evaluation tests PSCC: exact-match accuracy is 6.94 percentage points higher than A-Mem's, with 67.2% of its API-token use.

Manuscript under review. The manuscript and code remain private.

Age-Biased Technological Change

Age-biased technological change is easy to name and hard to measure. As the project's sole RA, I link CPS, PSID, NLSY, O*NET, and PATSTAT into worker, occupation, task, and patent data. I then carry those measures into wage and sorting models, share/log-share GMM, a 999-replication block bootstrap, and calibration checks. The technology measure draws on 60+ O*NET releases and about 56 million PATSTAT records.

The project is led by J. Frank Li and Cheng Chen at UBC Sauder.

Manuscript · Under review · 2026

When Answers Surprise

An earnings-call answer may matter because it departs sharply from the conversation before it. With Jerrold Huang, I built a panel of about 31,000 deduplicated firm events to test whether that answer-level novelty predicts returns beyond earnings surprise. Time holdouts, placebo tests, and timing checks ask whether the result survives a different split or timing assumption.

This paper is coauthored with Jerrold Huang.

Research assistant · UBC Sauder · May 2026–Present

Financial Media Multimodal Analysis

The first question is not what a model finds in the news, but whether the news was assembled correctly. I reconciled 1,330 RavenPack publication values against 3,210,045 ProQuest articles from July 1861 to May 2026. For a separate 85,259-ID financial-creator directory, I built the multimodal collection and validation pipeline. Raw files, provenance, and reconciliation results remain inspectable before any downstream analysis.

I work with Allen Hu at UBC Sauder.

Computer Vision Teaching and Research Notebooks

On Mar 25, 2026, I designed, hosted, and taught a Praxis UBC workshop that followed an image from preprocessing and bag-of-visual-words through CNN/ViT embeddings, classification, object detection, OCR, and captioning. A Jul 25, 2025 notebook with Yash Mali and Krishaant Pathman used ConvNeXt V2 embeddings, PCA, clustering, and classification to examine Richter's kouroi categories.

Praxis work was supervised by Jonathan Graves and Laura Nelson. The image-embedding notebook is with Yash Mali and Krishaant Pathman.
