Nov 2025 - Dec 2025 / Causal ML / Course research project

Payment Friction and Spending: Double ML Evidence

A causal ML project on payment friction and daily spending in diary data.

  • Used DoubleML on 5,583 respondents and 22,332 in-person person-days from the 2024 Atlanta Fed Diary of Consumer Payment Choice.
  • Estimated that lower-friction payment mixes were associated with about 5% higher daily spending after high-dimensional controls; profiled CATEs by ticket size, income, and education.
