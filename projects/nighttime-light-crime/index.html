Illumi-Naughty: Nighttime Light, Moonlight, and Crime

A panel-data project that started with Vancouver neighborhoods and now extends to U.S. county-month crime, NASA Black Marble lights, and moonlight shocks.

Co-authored with Irene Berezin, Jerrold Huang, Jaehyung Park, and William Clinton Co.

ECON 398 repoReport PDFExtension repo

Sufficient statistics

The question
How does nighttime illumination relate to crime across neighborhoods and county-month panels?
The data
Vancouver neighborhood crime and census geography, plus an extension using U.S. county-month crime, VNP46A3 Black Marble NTL, moon illumination, night length, and satellite QA fields.
My part
I built the Vancouver geospatial panel and maps, then extended the design into a U.S. county-month setup using VNP46A3 lights, MoonExp, county fixed effects, and year-month fixed effects.
What came out
A Vancouver paper and notebooks, neighborhood maps, NTL/crime choropleths, regression panels, and an extension repo with dashboard code, county-month parquet data, moon-illumination utilities, and robustness plans.
Methods
census-tract panels, county-month panels, fixed effects, moonlight shocks, negative binomial models, geospatial joins, county FE, year-month FE, crime-category heterogeneity
Tech stack
Python, R, GeoPandas, panel data, fixed effects
