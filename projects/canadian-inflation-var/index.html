Canadian Inflation Forecasting and Macro Signal Evaluation

A Bank of Canada presentation project on Canadian CPI forecasting with parsimonious and extended VAR specifications.

Course project and presentation work; public materials are linked where available.

GitHubRendered deck

Sufficient statistics

The question
Which macro-financial indicators improve Canadian CPI forecasts, and how much model complexity is justified?
The data
Canadian macro and financial indicators, including CPI measures, CEER, money supply, unemployment gaps, overnight rates, and U.S. CPI.
My part
I built the R/Quarto RevealJS deck, compared parsimonious and extended VAR specifications, and interpreted rolling forecasts and impulse responses for a policy audience.
What came out
A source QMD deck, rendered HTML slides, forecast charts, rolling validation against naive benchmarks, model comparisons, and policy-facing interpretation.
Methods
stationarity checks, VAR specifications, AIC lag selection, rolling-window validation, benchmark forecasts, impulse responses
Tech stack
R, Quarto, VAR, time series, RevealJS
