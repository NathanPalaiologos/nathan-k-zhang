TOO LEGIT TO OVERFIT

Unofficial titles

  • Natural Language Processor
  • 21st Century Chūnibyō Man
  • Village Head of Aishantang

Zhang is a little too legit for his own good. He refuses to overfit, but high bias is not an acceptable exit either. His working solution is to keep adding variables that appear unrelated: political science, military affairs, history, science fiction, linguistics, film, video games, anime, and rock music. The limiting case is a very high-dimensional subspace.

Zhang does not believe in a free lunch. He has found few examples in the sample and therefore often skips lunch altogether. His diet is roughly 90% home cooking, 5% Tim Hortons, and 5% filed under "other."

To keep the sample from collapsing to one room, Zhang runs and cycles. He has covered a respectable share of Vancouver, especially UBC. The choice of sports can be traced to Run with the Wind and Nasu: Summer in Andalusia.

Zhang no longer disappears into video games as he did in high school. The latest exception was Death Stranding 2: On the Beach. He has since started making a game of his own. The project is not public. No Codec frequency has been assigned. Zhang accepts no liability for whether it ever ships.
