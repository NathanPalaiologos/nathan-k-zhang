About me

Zhang is a little too legit for his own good. He refuses to overfit, but he is equally unwilling to accept high bias. To keep himself honest, he keeps adding variables that seem unrelated and expanding the sample. The usual dimensions include political science, military affairs, history, science fiction, linguistics, film, video games, anime, and rock music. At this rate, Zhang may one day span a very high-dimensional subspace.

Zhang does not believe in a free lunch. He has found very few in the sample, too, and therefore often skips lunch altogether. His diet is roughly 90% home cooking, 5% Tim Hortons, and 5% filed under "other."

To avoid shutting himself in a small room and calling it the whole world, Zhang goes out to run and cycle. He has now achieved a respectable map-completion rate across Vancouver, especially UBC. Why running and cycling? Credit Run with the Wind and Nasu: Summer in Andalusia.

Zhang no longer disappears into video games the way he did in high school. The most recent exception was Death Stranding 2: On the Beach. He has now decided to make a game of his own. The project is not yet public, and no Codec frequency has been assigned. Zhang accepts no contractual or legal liability for whether anything comes of it.
